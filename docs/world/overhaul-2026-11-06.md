# Overhaul, 6 November 2026: measurements

Lab numbers, Chromium (Playwright), production builds served locally.
"Before" is commit 03b7d0d built in `.playwright-mcp/before/` (port 3212);
"after" is the working tree (port 3211). Scripts:
`.playwright-mcp/overnight/scripts/perf*.js`, `throttled.js`; raw results:
`.playwright-mcp/overnight/perf/{before,after}.json`. These are lab
measurements and interaction proxies, not field data: nothing here is
real-user RUM.

"Words" = time from navigation start until the page's `h1` and all its
ancestors are at full opacity (the content is readable), polled every 16 ms.

## Scene initialisation

Total script time over the first 5 s on desktop `/` is unchanged (1.9 s
before and after): the park is built in code, and all of it is still
built. What changed is when: the far scenery (city, hills, far trees) and
the other five scenes are now built in idle time after the first frame,
so the first frame and the words come sooner; the workshop's nine
screenshots load on demand instead of at once (desktop `/` fetches no
images now: 221 KB → 0). The 281 KB (gzip) three.js chunk is the largest
remaining asset on wide screens.

## Core Web Vitals targets

LCP ≤ 2.5 s, CLS ≤ 0.1: every lab figure above is inside both (LCP worst
case 832 ms on throttled 3G). INP ≤ 200 ms: not measured as such; the
proxies are the warm-navigation figure (245–317 ms until the next page's
words are visible, which includes the route change and the entrance
animation) and the immediate selection in the timetable, cabinets and
bench tabs (store updates, no awaiting). Field data would be needed to
claim the targets are met for real visitors.
