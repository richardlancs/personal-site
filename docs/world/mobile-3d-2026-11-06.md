# The world on phones, 6 November 2026: measurements

Lab numbers from Playwright's Chromium on this Mac. **Its WebGL is
software (SwiftShader), with no GPU**, so anything GPU-bound (script time
during rendering, frame times) is much worse here than on a real phone,
and the frame-rate figures below say how the emulator copes, not how an
iPhone will. Nothing here is field data. Raw results:
`.playwright-mcp/overnight/perf/mobile-3d.json`; scripts
`.playwright-mcp/overnight/scripts/perf-mobile*.js`.

"Before" is f38a2dc (the painting layout, no three.js on phones);
"after" is branch `mobile-3d`. Phone 390×844 at 3× (canvas 487×1055 at
the 1.25 pixel-ratio cap).

## Cold loads, phone (quiet machine)

| Route | Transfer before → after | Words visible before → after | First world frame | LCP after | CLS |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `/` | 435 → 604 KB | 146 → 355 ms | 516 ms | 168 ms | 0 |
| `/projects` | 443 → 836 KB | 89 → 68 ms | 508 ms | 188 ms | 0 |
| `/about` | 404 → 576 KB | 94 → 59 ms | 450 ms | 172 ms | 0 |

The extra transfer is three.js and the scenes (JS 162 → 445 KB, gzip),
which phones now use; `/projects` also fetches the workshop's nine
monitor screenshots at 1200 px (the chosen project's first, the rest in
idle time). The painting (118 KB) is no longer fetched, except as the
no-WebGL fallback. The world mounts after `requestIdleCallback` on narrow
screens, so the sheet paints first; without that gate (first run) the
words waited 0.7–1.4 s behind scene building.

## Rendering, phone, CPU 4× slower (software WebGL)

| | Before (2D) | After (3D) |
|---|---|---|
| Frames in 3 s / average / p95 | 180 / 16.7 ms / 18.6 ms | 89 / 33.9 ms / 83.8 ms |
| JS heap at rest → after 14 navigations | 4 → 5 MB | 26 → 46 MB |

The heap growth is the other five scenes being built in idle time after
navigation (the same on desktop); it is bounded, not a leak, but on the
mobile tier it would be better not to prebuild them at all (see the
integration note). The frame figures are the emulator's software
rasteriser; a real phone GPU is not measurable from here.

## The mobile tier (World3D.tsx `detectQuality` → "mobile")

Pixel ratio capped at 1.25; the composer's target has 2× MSAA (the canvas
itself is created without antialiasing, since every frame goes through
the composer); 512 px shadow maps; no brushwork pass (the grade pass
stays: it's one cheap full-screen read); `powerPreference: "default"`;
scenes build at their "low" tier (16 trees, 12 000 grass blades, 500
fallen and 16 falling leaves, 50 dust motes); drag-to-look is off on
coarse pointers; the frame loop stops when the tab is hidden, the world
is off screen, or the sheet has been scrolled past 1.05× the viewport
height; the Director lifts the subject by 20% of the view on narrow
screens and uses a 62° field of view in portrait.

## Verified by hand

Screenshots in `.playwright-mcp/overnight/mobile3d/`: every route at 390
(`m3d-<route>-390.png`, plus `-full` for the whole page), 320, 430, a
landscape phone (844×390) and a portrait tablet (768×1024). Checked: no
horizontal overflow anywhere; no console errors; the canvas is 1.25× on
phones; tapping a bench tab chooses the project and sets `#scrollganizer`;
scrolling the sheet past a screen pauses the world and scrolling back
resumes it; Copy buttons and the résumé link are present on Contact; name
tags under the sheet are hidden, and all tags are hidden on short
landscape screens. Not tested: Safari (iOS or macOS) and any real device
— only Chromium's emulation was available.
