# Shanghai Redesign, Plan B: The 3D City under `/city` — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** An opt-in, real-time 3D Shanghai at `/city`: a compressed riverside from which all six districts' signs can be seen and clicked, each district its own scene the camera travels into, every section's content in HTML panels over it, lit by Richmond Hill's clock and weather, and framed and coloured like the 2D plates.

**Architecture:** `/city` and `/city/[section]` are server-rendered pages whose HTML is the section's 2D content (the `city2d/sections/*` components) inside a panel, with the district's plate band under it. A client shell (`city3d/CityShell.tsx`) checks WebGL, Save-Data and the tier, then dynamically imports the 3D chunk (`city3d/City3D.tsx`): one canvas, the park's camera Director and store carried over, a new "city kit" (instanced towers with shader windows, four hand-built landmarks, neon and screens, wet streets and water, signs in English with a short fixed list of Chinese words), and the atmosphere driver retuned to the plates. The riverside (Home) lives in the 3D chunk; each of the five other districts is its own chunk, loaded on demand as `sceneChunks.ts` does today. Bloom (`@react-three/postprocessing`) runs on the desktop tiers only. Nothing under `city3d/`, `three`, R3F or postprocessing is reachable from `/`.

**Tech Stack:** Next.js 16.4 App Router, React 19.3, TypeScript, Tailwind 4, three r186 (`WebGLRenderer`, `three/addons` `Reflector`), @react-three/fiber 9.8, `postprocessing` 6.39.x and `@react-three/postprocessing` 3.2.x (3D chunk only), `node --test`, Playwright via `docs/audit/scripts/pw.mjs`. No drei: the one helper considered (`<Text>` for sign lettering) is not needed, since signs are canvas textures as today (`textures.ts: fonts()`), and its troika dependency would add ~40 KB gzip.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-19-shanghai-cyberpunk-design.md` ("The 3D version (opt-in)", "Settled after the builder's review" 3, 4, 7). Style: `docs/city/style.md`, the plates in `app/assets/city/`, the dusk frames in `docs/city/concepts/`.

## Scene architecture

**One world, places far apart.** As in the park: the riverside (Home) at the origin, each district at its own `offset` 2 km away, past the fog, so only one is ever drawn. `SceneId` becomes `DistrictId` (`lujiazui` is the riverside). The Director (from `World3D.tsx`) keeps its journey: from the riverside the camera flies a short path toward the district's sign (`routesFromRiverside`), a threshold closes in the district's colour, the district is swapped in, the camera settles at its `home` pose. District → district goes back through the riverside, as park → place → park did. Still view cuts.

**The compressed riverside (call).** The camera stands at the Bund railing looking east across a Huangpu compressed to ~140 m. Left to right, at distances chosen so each sign is at least 48 CSS px wide at 390px and nothing stands on its line of sight:

| Position | District | What stands there | Sign (English / Chinese) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Near bank, far left | longtang | A cluster of shikumen roofs, one lit upstairs window | "Longtang" / 弄堂 |
| Left, mid-river | metro | A Line 2 viaduct crossing the frame low, a train | "Line 2" / 2号线 |
| Centre, far bank | (home) | The Lujiazui skyline: the four landmarks, ~60 instanced towers | none; this is Home |
| Right of centre, far bank | towers | A lit walkway ring at the base of the three supertalls | "Century Avenue" / 世纪大道 |
| Near bank, right | nanjing-road | A neon street mouth between two old facades | "Nanjing Road" / 南京路 |
| Near bank, far right | bund | The Customs House clock tower, floodlit | "The Bund" / 外滩 |

Each sign is a 3D board (kit `buildSign`) with an HTML tag projected onto its anchor (`anchors.ts` + the label projector from `World3D.tsx`): the tag is the keyboard path and a second click target. Fog has a floor (`fog.keep`) past the signs; the skyline takes more fog than the signs (`fogScale`).

**Chunks.**
- `city3d` chunk (loaded by `CityShell` via `next/dynamic`, `ssr: false`): `City3D.tsx`, the kit, atmosphere, precipitation, post, `Riverside.tsx` (it is every journey's hub and uses every kit module).
- One chunk each: `districts/Longtang.tsx`, `Towers.tsx`, `Metro.tsx`, `NanjingRoad.tsx` (with `arcade/`), `Bund.tsx`. The address's district loads first; the others in idle time after arrival (desktop tiers build them; mobile fetches but builds none ahead), or on pointer-over/focus of a link to them.
- Pose definitions live in `districts/<id>.def.ts` (no three import) and are aggregated by `scenes.ts`, so the Director knows every pose without pulling district code into the main chunk.

**Lighting, per scene, fixed (call):** one directional (sun by day, moon by night, shadows on desktop tiers only), one hemisphere, and exactly `DISTRICT_LIGHTS[id]` spot lights (riverside 0, longtang 2, towers 2, metro 3, nanjing-road 3, bund 4) whose intensities are 0 by day and rise with `uLamps`. Windows, neon, screens, the Pearl's spheres and lamp heads are emissive surfaces, not lights. Light after dark that falls on surfaces nearby is an additive `wash` (kept from `glow.ts`).

**How the atmosphere maps onto the city.** `atmosphere/looks.ts` keeps its shape (DAYLIGHT × WEATHER, blended 3 s) with values retuned to the plates in Task 7, plus three new DaylightLook fields:

| Look | Drives | Day | Dusk (start → end) | Night | Weather on top |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `top`, `horizon` | sky dome | `#2c64c6` / `#a4cdee` (lujiazui-day) | amber band `#ffb454` at horizon → ink | `#0c1526` / `#1d2a5c` + magenta city glow low | cloudy/rain grey toward `--line`, snow lifts |
| `fog`, `fogNear/Far` | `scene.fog` | blue haze, far | warm → violet | `#141f3a`, nearer | rain ×0.55 range, snow ×0.6, cloudy ×0.8; `keep` floor always |
| `neon` (new) | `neonUniforms.uNeon`, bloom intensity | 0.3 (signs read as panels, as in the day plates) | rises with progress | 1.0 | rain/snow ×1.15 (haze spreads it) |
| `windows` (new, from the hour) | `windowUniforms.uLitShare` | `litShare(hour)` (≈0.04) | ≈0.4 | 0.75 at 21:00, 0.25 at 04:00 | none |
| `cityGlow` (new) | horizon glow colour on the dome | 0 | 0.4 | 1 | cloudy/rain ×1.3 (light pollution on cloud) |
| existing `sun`, `hemi*`, `night` | directional, hemisphere, `envUniforms.uNight` | as today's shape | | | `wet`, `snow`, `rain`, `flakes` unchanged |

The hour comes from Richmond Hill's clock (the site's one clock): `SkyDriver` writes `hour` into the store alongside `sky`.

**Reflection strategy per tier (call):** high: `planar`, one `Reflector` per scene at most (riverside river, Bund river, Nanjing Road paving), render target 512 px wide, reflecting a layer mask (towers, landmarks, neon, signs; not trees, rain or movers); low and mobile: `fake`, a fragment shader that streaks the scene's `LightStrip`s vertically with noise, scaled by `uWet` (paving) or always on (river). Metro, longtang and towers paving are `fake` on every tier.

## Renderer tiers and budgets

`city3d/tiers.ts` (pure, no three import) picks the tier and holds the numbers. Budgets are per frame at the district's home pose, measured by `renderer.info` through the development handle `window.__city`.

| | high | low | mobile |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who | desktop, discrete or Apple GPU, ≥ 8 cores | desktop with Intel/AMD integrated GPU (`WEBGL_debug_renderer_info`), ≤ 4 cores, `deviceMemory` ≤ 4, or Save-Data | coarse pointer or the narrow layout (`flat.ts` query) |
| DPR cap | 1.75 | 1.5 | 1.25 |
| Antialias | composer MSAA 4 | composer MSAA 2 | canvas `antialias: true`, no composer |
| Bloom | mipmap blur, 0.5 resolution, 5 levels, threshold 0.82 | 0.25 resolution, 4 levels | none; `glowSprites` |
| Reflection | planar 512 | fake | fake |
| Shadows | sun 2048, PCF, desktop districts only (not the riverside) | sun 1024 | none |
| Draw calls | ≤ 260 | ≤ 200 | ≤ 120 |
| Triangles | ≤ 1.2 M | ≤ 700 k | ≤ 350 k |
| Texture memory | ≤ 128 MB | ≤ 96 MB | ≤ 48 MB |
| Frame time p95 (target) | ≤ 16.7 ms at 1440×900 | ≤ 16.7 ms on Iris Xe class; proxy ≤ 11 ms on the dev machine with `?tier=low` | ≤ 33 ms on a mid-range phone; proxy ≤ 16.7 ms at 390×844 DPR 2 `?tier=mobile` |

Runtime step-down (call): if p90 frame time over a 3 s window exceeds 1.5× the tier's target after arrival, high drops to low, once per visit, never back up and never to mobile.

**Bundle budgets** (`docs/audit/scripts/budget.mjs`, gzip -9): `city3dChunk` (the chunk holding `WebGLRenderer`, plus the riverside) ≤ 330,000; each district chunk ≤ 32,000; `city3dTotalJs` ≤ 470,000; `/city` initial JS ≤ `pageInitialJs` (210,000) since the 3D chunk loads after the page; sign font ≤ 40,000 raw. "No three on `/`": every script `/`'s prerendered HTML loads, and every chunk named in its RSC payload, is scanned for `WebGLRenderer`, `@react-three` and `postprocessing` markers; any hit fails. Numbers set from the first measurement with 5% headroom may only go lower.

## Accessibility and fallbacks

- **Real HTML first.** Each `/city/*` page server-renders the section's 2D component inside `<main>` with one `h1` (home: `profile.name` from `Hero`; districts: the section title), the district's `DistrictBand`, and a "2D view" link. Readable without JS, WebGL or the 3D chunk. Words arrive within 0.1 s; the camera journey plays behind them.
- **Keyboard.** The riverside's six sign tags are `<Link>`s in DOM order left to right, after the panel; the Sections menu and `stations` list also reach every district. In districts, the pickers (projects, board lines, arcade modes) use `selector.ts` arrow keys. Escape on a district goes back to `/city`.
- **Focus on travel.** On a pathname change the panel's heading (`tabIndex={-1}`) takes focus immediately, not after the camera settles; the document title changes from metadata. No live region for the journey.
- **Still view and reduced motion.** Instant cuts; frame loop `demand`; movers parked (train at the platform, boats mid-river, aircraft lights on); arcade auto-advance off; rain as still strokes at half density (as today).
- **`prefers-contrast: more`.** Panels opaque `--panel`, text-shadows none, bloom intensity ×0.5, sign tags opaque.
- **WebGL missing.** On `/` the "3D city" link and the Interests "Play it in the 3D city" link stay `hidden`. A direct visit to `/city/*` shows the 2D station (band + content) and a line "Your browser can't draw the 3D city" with a link to the 2D page.
- **Context lost.** `webglcontextlost` → `preventDefault()`, the canvas is hidden and the band shown with "Restart the 3D city" (glass button); `webglcontextrestored` or the button remounts the canvas (key bump). After a second loss in a visit, the fallback stays.
- **Save-Data** (`navigator.connection.saveData`): `/city/*` shows the fallback and a "Load the 3D city (about N MB)" button; the size is written at build into `tiers.ts` from the budget. Nothing 3D is fetched until pressed.
- **Phones.** Mobile tier, no drag-to-look; the canvas fills the top, the page is a dark sheet (`--panel`) from `--hero` (46svh; 38svh on short screens) over the lower part; the frame loop stops once the sheet covers the canvas and when the tab is hidden.

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| **Landmark likeness from primitives** (the Pearl and Shanghai Tower are the ones visitors know) | Real proportions in Task 6; reference crops beside each build; Checkpoint 1 judges them before any district uses them; if the twist reads poorly, a stepped-rotation approximation with lit band edges (as the night plate draws it) |
| **Bloom cost on integrated GPUs** | Bloom at 0.25 resolution on low, none on mobile; one-time step-down; `city3d-perf.mjs` with `?tier=low` on every district; cut bloom from low entirely (glow sprites, as mobile) if Iris-class proxy fails |
| **Panel legibility over bright neon at night and snow glare by day** | Panels are near-opaque `--panel` (0.9) with blur, never clear glass; camera frames each district so its subject sits in the other half; `city3d-contrast.mjs` over 96 combinations; opaque under `prefers-contrast: more` |
| **The two versions drifting** | One content source (`city2d/sections/*` inside the panels), one registry, one style sheet; `plate-compare.mjs` fails on colour drift; the 2D↔3D link round-trip test |
| **Wrong or awkward Chinese** | A short list of real names and plain words; every entry `checked: false` until Richard confirms; unconfirmed entries render English only (`buildSign` ignores `zh` whose `checked` is false) |
| **3D weight leaking to `/`** | The import-graph test, `prefetch={false}`, the static scan in `budget.mjs`, the network check in `city3d-perf.mjs` |
| **`@react-three/postprocessing` 3.2 with three r186 / fiber 9.8** | Peers allow it (three ≥0.168 <0.188, fiber ≥9.7); pin minors; if it misbehaves, use `postprocessing`'s `EffectComposer` directly in `Post.tsx` (same chunk, no wrapper) |
| **Phone memory and heat** | Mobile tier builds only the district showing; textures ≤ 48 MB; stop the loop under the sheet and when hidden; Richard's real-device check at Checkpoint 3 |
| **Scope** | The largest parts, in order: the five districts (Tasks 10–13), the riverside (Task 8), the kit's window shader and reflections (Task 5), plate matching (Task 14). **Cut first, in this order:** planar reflections (fake everywhere); aircraft lights and river traffic; the train's motion (parked train); drag-to-look; screenshots on the project screens (title cards instead); then simplify Longtang and the Bund to the riverside vignettes seen closer. Never cut: the HTML panels, the fallbacks, the budgets, the visibility rule. |
