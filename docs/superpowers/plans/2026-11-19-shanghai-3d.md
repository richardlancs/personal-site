# Shanghai Redesign, Plan B: The 3D City under `/city` — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** An opt-in, real-time 3D city at `/city`: a compressed riverside (Home) from which the five other sections' signs (About, Projects, Experience, Interests, Contact) can be seen and clicked, each section its own scene the camera travels into, every section's content in HTML panels over it, lit by Richmond Hill's clock and weather, and framed and coloured like the 2D plates.

**Architecture:** `/city` and five static pages `/city/<section>` are server-rendered; their HTML is the section's 2D content (the `city2d/sections/*` components, unchanged in content) in a panel, over a fixed plate band. `app/city/layout.tsx` mounts a client shell (`city3d/CityShell.tsx`) that checks WebGL2, Save-Data and the tier, then imports the 3D chunk (`city3d/City3D.tsx`) with `next/dynamic` (`ssr: false`): one canvas kept across `/city/*` navigations, the park's Director carried over, a new city kit (instanced towers with shader windows, four landmarks, neon and screens, wet streets and water, signs that carry section names in English and otherwise only abstract shapes), and the atmosphere driver retuned to the plates. The riverside lives in the 3D chunk; each other section's scene is its own chunk, loaded on demand. Bloom (`@react-three/postprocessing`) runs on the desktop tiers only. Nothing under `city3d/` (except `store.ts`), `three`, R3F or postprocessing is reachable from `/`.

**Tech Stack:** Next.js 16.4.6 App Router (`cacheComponents`, `partialPrefetching`), React 19.3.0, TypeScript, Tailwind 4, three 0.186.1 (WebGL2 only; `three/addons/objects/Reflector.js`), @react-three/fiber 9.8.2, `postprocessing` ~6.39.6 and `@react-three/postprocessing` ~3.2.2 (3D chunk only), `node --test`, Playwright via `docs/audit/scripts/pw.mjs`. No drei: signs are canvas textures (`kit/textures.ts`), and drei's `<Text>` would bring troika.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-19-shanghai-cyberpunk-design.md` ("The 3D version (opt-in)", "Settled after the builder's review" 3, 7), as amended by Richard's decisions below (his words win). ## Scene architecture

**One world, places far apart.** As in the park: the riverside (Home) at the origin, each section's scene at its own `offset` 2 km away, past the fog, so only one is drawn; `Warm` precompiles each place alone, as the park's did. `SceneId = DistrictId` (`lujiazui` is the riverside). The Director (from the park's `World3D.tsx`) keeps its journey: from the riverside the camera flies toward the section's sign (`routesFromRiverside`), a threshold closes in the scene's colour, the scene is swapped in, the camera settles at its `home` pose. Section to section goes back through the riverside. Still view cuts. The scene comes from the pathname: `sectionForCityPath(pathname)` (Task 2) then the registry's `district`.

**The compressed riverside (call).** The camera stands at a river railing looking across a river compressed to ~140 m. Left to right, at distances chosen so each sign is at least 48 CSS px wide at 390px and nothing stands on its line of sight:

| Position | Section (district key) | What stands there | Sign |
|---|---|---|---|
| Near bank, far left | About (`longtang`) | A cluster of stone-gate lane roofs, one lit upstairs window | "About" |
| Left, mid-river | Experience (`metro`) | An elevated rail viaduct crossing the frame low, a train | "Experience" |
| Centre, far bank | Home (`lujiazui`) | The skyline: the four landmarks, ~60 instanced towers | none; this is Home |
| Right of centre, far bank | Projects (`towers`) | A lit walkway ring at the base of the three supertalls | "Projects" |
| Near bank, right | Interests (`nanjing-road`) | A neon street mouth between two old facades | "Interests" |
| Near bank, far right | Contact (`bund`) | A floodlit clock tower | "Contact" |

Each sign is a 3D board (`kit/signs.ts: buildSign`) with the section's `title` and an HTML tag projected onto its anchor (`anchors` key `sign:<sectionId>`, the park's `LabelProjector`/`Labels` pattern): the tag is a `<Link>` to `cityPath(id)`, the keyboard path and a second click target. Fog has a floor (`fog.keep`) past the signs; the skyline takes more fog (`fogScale`). On portrait phones the projector stacks the five tags in two rows by alternate height; the visibility script checks 8px gaps.

**Chunks.**
- `city3d` chunk (`CityShell` → `next/dynamic(() => import("./City3D"), { ssr: false })`): `City3D.tsx`, the kit, atmosphere, precipitation, `Post.tsx`, `Riverside.tsx`.
- One chunk each: `districts/{longtang,towers,metro,nanjing-road,bund}.tsx` (lowercase id file names; default exports `AboutScene`, `ProjectsScene`, `ExperienceScene`, `InterestsScene`, `ContactScene`). The address's scene loads first; the others in idle time after arrival (desktop tiers build them; mobile fetches but builds none ahead), or on pointer-over or focus of a link to them.
- Poses live in `districts/<id>.def.ts` (no three import), aggregated by `scenes.ts`, so the Director knows every pose without pulling scene code into the main chunk.

**Lighting, per scene, fixed (call):** one directional (sun by day, moon by night; shadows on desktop section scenes only, `SunLight` from the park's `scenes/common.tsx`), one hemisphere, and exactly `DISTRICT_LIGHTS[id]` spot lights (riverside 0, longtang 2, towers 2, metro 3, nanjing-road 3, bund 4) whose intensities are 0 by day and rise with night. Windows, neon, screens, the landmarks' lit parts and lamp heads are emissive surfaces, not lights. Light on nearby surfaces after dark is an additive `wash` (kept from `glow.ts`).

## Accessibility and fallbacks

- **Real HTML first.** Each `/city/*` page server-renders `<main className="city-page" data-section={id}>`: the band, then `.city-panel` holding one `h1` (home: `Hero`'s `h1#home-title`; others: the section's `title`, id `<id>-title`), the section component with `where="3d"` (inner headings one level up, Task 3), a "2D view" text link to `twoDHref`, and `<Colophon />` (the weather credit is on every page). No `#home` element, so the progress line hides itself. Readable without JS, WebGL or the 3D chunk.
- **Layout.** The page scrolls; the canvas never does. Wide screens: the canvas is `position: fixed` under the bar across the screen, the panel takes the left half (content centred in it, as the park's `.location`), each scene's `frame: "right"`. Narrow layout (`FLAT_QUERY`): the canvas fills the top `--hero` (46svh, 38svh under `max-height: 480px`), the panel is a `--panel` sheet over the rest; the loop stops once the sheet covers the canvas.
- **No layout shift.** The band (`.city-band`) is `position: fixed` in the canvas's box; the canvas fades in over it and the band is hidden (`html[data-city3d="on"] .city-band { visibility: hidden }`).
- **Keyboard.** The riverside's five sign tags are `<Link>`s rendered after the canvas; the Sections menu (city paths on `/city/*`) reaches every section; on `/city`, `Hero`'s two actions link to `/city/projects` and `/city/contact`. In section scenes the pickers use `panels/selector.ts` (`stepChoice`, `onChoiceKey`). Escape in a section scene goes to `/city`.
- **Focus on travel.** On a pathname change after the first render, the panel's heading (`tabIndex={-1}`) takes focus at once (`preventScroll`), not when the camera settles; never on first load. No live region for the journey.
- **Still view.** Instant cuts, no parallax; `frameloop` `demand`; movers `park()`ed; arcade auto-advance off; rain as still strokes at half density.
- **`prefers-contrast: more`.** Panels opaque `--panel`, text-shadows none, `<Post>` without `Bloom`, no glow sprites, tags opaque.
- **WebGL2 missing.** On `/` the "3D city" key and the Interests link stay `visibility: hidden` (space kept) until `html[data-webgl2]`; a direct visit to `/city/*` shows the band and content and "Your browser can't draw the 3D city" with a link to the 2D page.
- **Context lost.** `webglcontextlost` → `preventDefault()`, `data-city3d="lost"`, the band shows with "Restart the 3D city" (glass key); `webglcontextrestored` or the key remounts the canvas (key bump). After a second loss in a visit, the fallback stays.
- **Save-Data** (`navigator.connection.saveData`): `/city/*` shows the fallback and a "Load the 3D city (about N MB)" glass key; N is `SAVE_DATA_MB` in `tiers.ts`, updated from the Task 19 budget run. Nothing 3D is fetched until pressed.
- **Covered.** The loop stops (`frameloop="never"`) when the tab is hidden, when the sheet covers the canvas, and while `html.resume-lock` is set (a `MutationObserver` on `<html>`'s class in `City3D`).

**Shared shader state, `city3d/kit/uniforms.ts`** (the only shared mutable shader state; kit and atmosphere import from here only)
- `neonUniforms = { uNeon: { value: 0.3 } }`: written by `atmosphere/Atmosphere.tsx`; read by `kit/neon.ts`.
- `windowUniforms = { uLitShare, uWarm, uCool, uFloor, uBay }` (`uLitShare` starts at `litShare(12)`, `uWarm #ffc98a`, `uCool #bfe4ff`, `uFloor 0.36`, `uBay 0.3`): `uLitShare` written by the atmosphere from the store's `hour`; read by `kit/windows.ts`.

**Materials, `city3d/kit/materials.ts`** (one cache for the canvas; disposal is central)
- `shared<T extends THREE.Material>(key: string, make: () => T): T`: only the kit's factories call it.
- `disposeShared(): void`: called by `City3D.tsx` when the canvas unmounts. Scenes dispose their own geometry and textures, never a shared material.
- `sharedCount(): number`; `invisibleMaterial(): THREE.MeshBasicMaterial` (the Hitbox's).
- Placeholder factories with the plan's signatures, bodies plain until their task fills them (callers never change): `kit/surface.ts: surface(o: { color; map?; wet?; snow?; grime? }): MeshLambertMaterial` (Task 6); `kit/windows.ts: windowMaterial(o: { base; glass; far? }): MeshLambertMaterial` (Task 6); `kit/neon.ts: NEON_HDR = 3`, `SCENE_TONES { cyan #4fd8ee, rose #f062b4, gold #ffb454 }`, `type SceneTone`, `neonMaterial(tone, o?: { dayColor? }): MeshBasicMaterial`, `screenMaterial(draw, o: { w; h; key }): MeshBasicMaterial` (its `userData.redraw()` paints the canvas again, e.g. once a font has loaded) (Task 7). **The scene contract, `city3d/common.tsx`** (documented at the top of the file)

### Added after wave 4 (the integration, 20 November 2026)

What the five scenes asked of the shell and the kit (report: `.superpowers/sdd/2026-11-19-shanghai-3d/wave4-integration-report.md`). Tested in `tests/city3d-contract.test.mjs` and `tests/city3d-kit-wave4.test.mjs`.

**Shell**
- **Focus poses are followed.** The Director rests on `restingPose(id, world.focus)` (`scenes.ts`): the def's `focus[key]` when the store's `focus` names one of the place's, else `home`. A change of `focus` turns the camera there (or back) on `focusTravel(from, to)`: a short arc through a point lifted 8% of the way (≤ 0.6 units), eased in and out, 0.6–1.1 s; a new choice on the way turns from wherever the camera is; never in the middle of a journey (its end settles on it); a cut in still view. A journey to another place clears `focus`. Nothing in the canvas moves keyboard focus.
- **`?frame=plate` (development only).** `/city/<section>?frame=plate` holds the place's `plateFrame` (pose and lens), letterboxes the canvas to the plates' shape (`PLATE_ASPECT`, 2400 × 1032) and hides the panel, the bar and the tags. Behind `process.env.NODE_ENV === "development"`: a production build has no such switch. Use with `?sky=` and wait ten seconds before a screenshot.
- **`Hitbox` says where it was hit:** `onSelect(hit: Hit)`, `Hit = { uv: Vector2 | null; point: Vector3 }`, `point` in the box's own frame, each axis −0.5..0.5 of its size.
- **`SceneDef.shelter?: { min: V3; max: V3 }`** (place coordinates): no rain or snow inside it (Experience: `METRO.shelter`). **`SceneDef.sun?: V3`**: the direction toward the sun for that place (the atmosphere moves the one directional light; no light added).
- **`useDisposeOnUnmount(built)`** (`common.tsx`): a scene's built resources are disposed when it really unmounts, not on development's double effect; a kit `dispose()` frees GPU resources and never detaches a mesh.

**Kit** (every material through `shared(materialKey(…))`, one program per kind, uniforms only)
- Light pools (`kit/glow.ts`: `GlowPool`, `setPools`, `clearPools`; `common.tsx: useGlowPools(id, pools, active)`): up to `POOLS` (12) per place, added to the lit surface after dark as light, not lights. Driven by the atmosphere (`poolUniforms.uPoolsOn`, with the lamps), × `glowOn`. The atmosphere now also drives `glowUniforms` (`uLamps`, `uSigns`, `uHaze`).
- Day shade (`kit/glow.ts`: `DayShade`, `setShade`, `clearShade`; `common.tsx: useDayShade(id, shade, active)`): stripes, dapple or an edge, laid on the lit surface by day (`shadeUniforms.uShadeOn`, the sun's share of the light).
- `surface({ …, relief?, glow? })`: `relief` `{ kind: "stone", bay, floor }` (pilasters, cornices, window surrounds) or `{ kind: "brick", course, length }` (staggered courses, mortar), in the shader; `glow` `{ tone, strength }` (floodlit after dark, as `litSurface`).
- `glassMaterial({ tint, opacity?, sky?, glow?, double? })` (`kit/glass.ts`).
- `pavingTexture({ seed, kind?: "slabs" | "grid" | "setts", cols?, rows?, joint?, repeat? })` (`kit/textures.ts`): one 256² tile per kind and seed; a `repeat` returns a clone sharing its upload (the caller disposes the clone).
- `clockFace(hour)` and `setClockHour(hour)` (`kit/textures.ts`), `clockMaterial(hour)` (`kit/neon.ts`).
- `buildPlanters(seed, spots, { stone? })` (`kit/trees.ts`): three draw calls; the trees' canopies are three or four merged masses with a lighter top.
- `screenMaterial`'s `userData.release()` / `restore()`: a screen's picture freed while its place isn't showing.

### Added in the second kit pass (after the gate 3 review, 20 November 2026)

What the review's ranked list asked for (report: `.superpowers/sdd/2026-11-19-shanghai-3d/kit2-report.md`). Tested in `tests/city3d-kit2.test.mjs`. Every option is a uniform: no new program, nothing compiles between day, night and weathers; a caller passing no new option gets what it got before.

**Shell**
- **`SceneDef.bounce?: { color; strength }`**: a day fill on what faces away from the place's sun (`bounceUniforms` in `kit/uniforms.ts`, written by the atmosphere × the sun's share of the light, read by the lit surface). Interests, About, Projects.
- **`SceneDef.moon?: false`**: no moon disc nor moon glow in that place after dark (Projects).

**Kit**
- `wetPaving({ …, polish?, glow?, snow?, grime? })` (`kit/water.ts`): `polish` (0..1) is polished stone's dry shine, the paving's own streaks at `polish × uNight` (none by day; in rain the wetter of the two, never both; as strong on high, where the planar mirror stays off while dry), drawn as long fine strokes (`uFine`: narrower, finer ripples, longer core, `uStretch` 1.7, running on to 0.35 units from the eye's feet). `glow`, `snow`, `grime` go to its `surface`. `buildStreaks(…, { polish? })` (paving kind only). Every streak now has a least near length (`max(D − 1, 0.25 D)`).
- `windowMaterial({ …, lit?, warm?, cool?, coolShare? })` (`kit/windows.ts`): the least share of windows lit after dark, the lit tones, the cool share (0.07 by default); per material (`windowVariant`), program `win:near` / `win:far` as before.
- `buildTowers(specs, { far, fogScale?, palette?: (t) => TowerPalette })` (`kit/towers.ts`): a facade per tower (`TowerPalette` = `WindowOptions` but `far`, `fogScale`), one mesh per style and facade.
- `buildStreetTrees(seed, spots: { x; z; s; kind?: "round" | "tall" | "plane" | "leafy" }[], { greens?, underGlow?: { tone; strength }, leaves? })` (`kit/trees.ts`): kinds per spot (the seed still picks round or tall where none is given, the same trees as before); plane trees on their own branched trunk (+2 draws), leafy clumps without a trunk (+1); greens as instance colours; the canopy's under-glow and leaf clusters as uniforms (program `tree:canopy`).
- `GlowPool` may be a line: `{ from; to; reach; color; strength }` (`kit/glow.ts`): the same light along a segment, at the cost of one of the 12 pools (`poolUniforms.uPoolEnds`).
- `buildTrain(cars, { span?, period?, nose?: "flat" | "round", snout?, shell?: SurfaceOptions, stripe?: { day; night }, windows?: "band" | "framed" })` (`kit/movers.ts`) returns the mover and `parts` (`cars`, `shell`, `windows`, `stripe`, `nose`, `screen`, `lamps`, `coupler`, named `train:*`); `trainLength(cars)`.
- `surface({ glow: { tone, strength, base?, fade? } })`: floodlit from the foot, full at `base`, a fifth `fade` higher.
- `buildMarkSign({ …, kind: "panel", arms? })` (`kit/signs.ts`, `MarkKind`): a plain glowing rectangle, both faces, a hair-thin rim, no edge, no bars, no picture (the neon material at half strength, pale `#e6e4de` by day); on a wall bracket unless `arms: false`.
- `buildLampRow(spots, { light?, head?: "arm" | "lantern" })` (`kit/street.ts`): a lantern post, its light in the lantern.
- `DayShade { kind: "lattice"; period; period2; duty; warm? }` (`kit/glow.ts`): stripes both ways; `warm` tints the sunlit share (`shadeUniforms.uShadeMore`).
