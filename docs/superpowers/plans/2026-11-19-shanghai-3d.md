# Shanghai Redesign, Plan B: The 3D City under `/city` — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** An opt-in, real-time 3D Shanghai at `/city`: a compressed riverside from which all five other districts' signs can be seen and clicked, each district its own scene the camera travels into, every section's content in HTML panels over it, lit by Richmond Hill's clock and weather, and framed and coloured like the 2D plates.

**Architecture:** `/city` and five static pages `/city/<section>` are server-rendered; their HTML is the section's 2D content (the `city2d/sections/*` components) inside a panel, over a fixed plate band. `app/city/layout.tsx` mounts a client shell (`city3d/CityShell.tsx`) that checks WebGL2, Save-Data and the tier, then imports the 3D chunk (`city3d/City3D.tsx`) with `next/dynamic` (`ssr: false`): one canvas kept across `/city/*` navigations, the park's Director carried over, a new "city kit" (instanced towers with shader windows, four landmarks, neon and screens, wet streets and water, signs in English with a short fixed list of Chinese words), and the atmosphere driver retuned to the plates. The riverside (Home) lives in the 3D chunk; each other district is its own chunk, loaded on demand as the park's `sceneChunks.ts` did. Bloom (`@react-three/postprocessing`) runs on the desktop tiers only. Nothing under `city3d/`, `three`, R3F or postprocessing is reachable from `/`.

**Tech Stack:** Next.js 16.4.6 App Router (`cacheComponents`, `partialPrefetching`), React 19.3.0, TypeScript, Tailwind 4, three 0.186.1 (WebGL2 only; `three/addons/objects/Reflector.js`), @react-three/fiber 9.8.2, `postprocessing` ~6.39.6 and `@react-three/postprocessing` ~3.2.2 (3D chunk only), `node --test`, Playwright via `docs/audit/scripts/pw.mjs`. No drei: signs are canvas textures (`kit/textures.ts`), and drei's `<Text>` would bring troika.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-19-shanghai-cyberpunk-design.md` ("The 3D version (opt-in)", "Settled after the builder's review" 3, 4, 7). Style: `docs/city/style.md`, the plates in `app/assets/city/` (2400×1032, 21:9), the frames in `docs/city/concepts/`.

## Scene architecture

**One world, places far apart.** As in the park: the riverside (Home) at the origin, each district at its own `offset` 2 km away, past the fog, so only one is drawn (hidden groups' lights are not counted, so each place's programs differ; `Warm` precompiles each place alone, as the park's did). `SceneId = DistrictId` (`lujiazui` is the riverside). The Director (from the park's `World3D.tsx`) keeps its journey: from the riverside the camera flies toward the district's sign (`routesFromRiverside`), a threshold closes in the district's colour, the district is swapped in, the camera settles at its `home` pose. District → district goes back through the riverside. Still view cuts. Today the park picked its scene from the pathname through `world/park.ts: parkSectionForPath`; the city uses `sectionForCityPath` (Task 2) and the registry's `district`.

**The compressed riverside (call).** The camera stands at the Bund railing looking east across a Huangpu compressed to ~140 m. Left to right, at distances chosen so each sign is at least 48 CSS px wide at 390px and nothing stands on its line of sight:

| Position | District | What stands there | Sign (English / Chinese) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Near bank, far left | longtang | A cluster of shikumen roofs, one lit upstairs window | "Longtang" / 弄堂 |
| Left, mid-river | metro | A Line 2 viaduct crossing the frame low, a train | "Line 2" / 2号线 |
| Centre, far bank | (home) | The Lujiazui skyline: the four landmarks, ~60 instanced towers | none; this is Home |
| Right of centre, far bank | towers | A lit walkway ring at the base of the three supertalls | "Century Avenue" / 世纪大道 |
| Near bank, right | nanjing-road | A neon street mouth between two old facades | "Nanjing Road" / 南京路 |
| Near bank, far right | bund | The Customs House clock tower, floodlit | "The Bund" / 外滩 |

Each sign is a 3D board (`kit/signs.ts: buildSign`) with an HTML tag projected onto its anchor (`anchors` key `river:<district>`, the park's `LabelProjector`/`Labels` pattern with its `TAG_GAP` collision rule): the tag is the keyboard path and a second click target. Fog has a floor (`fog.keep`) past the signs; the skyline takes more fog (`fogScale`). On portrait phones five tags cannot sit in one row of 44px keys: the projector stacks them in two rows by alternate height, and the visibility script checks 8px gaps.

**Chunks.**
- `city3d` chunk (`CityShell` → `next/dynamic(() => import("./City3D"), { ssr: false })`): `City3D.tsx`, the kit, atmosphere, precipitation, `Post.tsx`, `Riverside.tsx`.
- One chunk each: `districts/Longtang.tsx`, `Towers.tsx`, `Metro.tsx`, `NanjingRoad.tsx` (with `arcade/`), `Bund.tsx`. The address's district loads first; the others in idle time after arrival (desktop tiers build them; mobile fetches but builds none ahead), or on pointer-over/focus of a link to them.
- Poses live in `districts/<id>.def.ts` (no three import), aggregated by `scenes.ts`, so the Director knows every pose without pulling district code into the main chunk.

**Lighting, per scene, fixed (call):** one directional (sun by day, moon by night; shadows on desktop districts only, `SunLight` from the park's `scenes/common.tsx`), one hemisphere, and exactly `DISTRICT_LIGHTS[id]` spot lights (riverside 0, longtang 2, towers 2, metro 3, nanjing-road 3, bund 4) whose intensities are 0 by day and rise with night. Windows, neon, screens, the Pearl's spheres and lamp heads are emissive surfaces, not lights. Light falling on nearby surfaces after dark is an additive `wash` (kept from `glow.ts`).

**How the atmosphere maps onto the city.** `atmosphere/looks.ts` keeps its shape (`DAYLIGHT` start/end rows × `WEATHER`, `daylightLook(out, phase, progress)`, `weatherLook(out, preset)`, `fogRange(out, base, d, w, indoor)`, blended 3 s), its values retuned to the plates in Task 9, plus three new `DaylightLook` fields:

**Reflection strategy per tier (call):** high: `planar`, one `Reflector` per scene at most (riverside river, Bund river, Nanjing Road paving), `textureWidth` 512, `multisample: 0`, its reflection camera's layers set to `REFLECT_LAYER` only, drawn through a custom `shader` option that mixes the reflection with the water/paving colour and the fog; low and mobile: `fake`, a fragment shader that streaks the scene's `LightStrip`s vertically with noise, scaled by `uWet` (paving) or always on (river). ## Accessibility and fallbacks

- **Real HTML first.** Each `/city/*` page server-renders `<main className="city-page" data-district>`: the band, then a `CityPanel` holding one `h1` (home: `profile.name` from `Hero`, id `home-title`; districts: the section title, id `<id>-title`), then the section component with its inner headings at level 2 (`level={2}`, Task 2), and a "2D view" link. Readable without JS, WebGL or the 3D chunk.
- **No layout shift.** The band (`.city-band`) is `position: fixed` in the box the canvas will take; the canvas fades in over it and the band is hidden (`html[data-city3d="on"] .city-band { visibility: hidden }`), so nothing moves.
- **Keyboard.** The riverside's five sign tags are `<Link>`s rendered by `City3D` after the canvas; the Sections menu (city paths on `/city/*`) and `Hero`'s station index (city paths on `/city`) also reach every district. In districts the pickers use `panels/selector.ts` (`stepChoice`, `onChoiceKey`). Escape on a district goes to `/city`.
- **Focus on travel.** On a pathname change after the first render, the panel's heading (`tabIndex={-1}`) takes focus at once (`preventScroll`), not when the camera settles; never on first load. The title comes from metadata. No live region for the journey.
- **Still view.** Instant cuts, no parallax; `frameloop` `demand`; movers `park()`ed (train at the platform, boats mid-river, aircraft lights on); arcade auto-advance off; rain as still strokes at half density (as the park did).
- **`prefers-contrast: more`.** Panels opaque `--panel`, text-shadows none, `<Post>` without `Bloom`, no glow sprites, sign tags opaque.
- **WebGL2 missing** (three r163+ is WebGL2 only). On `/` the "3D city" key and the Interests link are `visibility: hidden` (their space kept); a direct visit to `/city/*` shows the band and content and "Your browser can't draw the 3D city" with a link to the 2D page.
- **Context lost.** `webglcontextlost` → `preventDefault()`, `data-city3d="lost"`, the band shows with "Restart the 3D city" (glass button); `webglcontextrestored` or the button remounts the canvas (key bump). After a second loss in a visit, the fallback stays.
- **Save-Data** (`navigator.connection.saveData`, call): `/city/*` shows the fallback and "Load the 3D city (about N MB)"; N is a constant in `tiers.ts` updated from the Task 17 budget run. Nothing 3D is fetched until pressed.
- **Phones.** Mobile tier, no drag-to-look; the canvas fills the top `--hero` (new token in `city.css`: 46svh, 38svh under `max-height: 480px`), the page is a dark sheet (`--panel`) over the rest; the frame loop stops once the sheet covers the canvas, when the tab is hidden, and under the résumé (`covered`).
