# UX review panel: the full fix list (23 November 2026)

Live site at `main` c77f82c. The review was done by four reviewers, each with its own view:

| Prefix | View |
|---|---|
| R | A recruiter with 60 seconds |
| A11Y | Accessibility |
| M | Phones, touch and performance |
| V | Visual and interaction craft |

Each reviewer audited the live site in a real browser at 390×844, 844×390, 667×375, 768×1024, 1024×768 and 1440×900. Each then read the other three audits and argued in a debate round.

- **Findings:** 65 in total, 1 Blocker, 21 Major, 28 Minor and 15 Nit. The audits and debates are in `.superpowers/sdd/2026-11-22-horizontal-city/ux-panel/`.
- **Richard's asks stand:** centred, substantial panels; clear glass; the motion blur; Experience as one stop; wider boxes that snap to centre. The debate settled only how to deliver them.
- **Floors kept:** 4.5:1 contrast for every word, no scroll-jacking, still view motionless, nothing invented.

## Priority 1: do first (all four reviewers agree)

### 1. Snap to centred panels (Richard's item, plus R1, R2, M2, A11Y-01, A11Y-04, A11Y-07, V6, V19)

- **Wider boxes:** every project box and the Experience box become significantly wider, about 80vw, within the central 80%.
- **Snap targets:** one invisible snap target per panel (each stop, each project, Skills, Experience as one stop), placed at the scroll position where that panel is centred. They come from the same position list CityTrack already computes.
- **Wide screens:** `scroll-snap-type: y proximity`, `scroll-snap-stop: normal`. A nudge settles on a panel; a fling passes through.
- **Narrow layout (phones, portrait tablets):** `y mandatory`, `scroll-snap-stop: always`. One swipe moves one panel, so nothing is ever left half on screen.
- **Remove the JS fling.** It moved 822px in one flick.
- **One list drives everything:** ArrowLeft/Right, PageDown/Space and the bar's current-section label all use the same list. The arrows now step through each project and Skills (A11Y-04). The label switches when a panel's centre passes the middle of the screen (V19).
- **Dropped:** stretching each stop's scroll distance on phones (R2's Round-1 idea). `snap-stop: always` does that job.

### 2. Panel layout: centred, substantial, sized by content (V1, V3, R8, A11Y-08, R13)

- **Shape:** every panel is centred under one shared top line, grows downward, and ends above the metro map (8px clear).
- **Size:** Projects and Experience are wide. Home, the Projects index, Interests and Contact are sized to their content but still substantial, with a minimum width so 200% text has room.
- **Stable heading:** the heading no longer jumps 50–100px from stop to stop, and opening a project's details no longer moves its title.
- **Dropped:** V2's proposal to push panels down to show more city. It conflicts with "substantial" and with 200% text; it was withdrawn in the debate.

### 3. Give the words the screen on phones (R7, M4, M7, V7, A11Y-02 in part)

- A one-row bar: the weather is compacted or moves into the menu.
- A shorter painting strip, with no dead dark band between the painting and the sheet.
- Size from `svh` rather than `dvh`, so the address bar showing or hiding doesn't resize the page.
- **Target:** the sheet gets 60% or more of the screen. Today it gets 49%.

### 4. Short screens and 200% text (A11Y-02, M3, V5, R11)

- **Screens 500px or less tall:** a compact heading, Home's actions in one row right after the name, and the map as dots.
- **Fallback:** if a panel would still be under about 20em tall, read the stacked top-to-bottom page instead. This extends the 22em rule already in place.

### 5. Home says who and what at once (R5, R6, V8)

- **Headline:** the role sought, in plain words, replaces the cyan "cs@uwaterloo" that reads like a broken email. Use Richard's own line from `portfolio.ts`: "Looking for a software engineering internship or co-op." Nothing invented.
- **Actions:** "View projects" (lantern) with a glass "Résumé" button beside it.
- **Cleanup:** remove the black box behind the name inside the glass, and the duplicate Contact/Email text links. Use one icon style.

### 6. Project panels on phones and tablets (R3, M12, V17, V10, M11)

- **Order:** title, then Live demo and Code, then the summary, then the cover capped at about 40% of the sheet. Do this in the DOM, not with CSS `order`, which would break reading and Tab order.
- **Wide screens:** the cover matches the text's height.
- **Extra screenshots:** they open readable, not as 128×80 thumbnails.
- **Tablet index:** don't truncate descriptions while the panel sits half empty.

### 7. Panels that scroll inside: visible, lossless, keyboard-reachable (A11Y-12, A11Y-03, A11Y-06, A11Y-10, M1, M5, M6, R4)

- **Fix Home's 9px overflow** so the first swipe on Home moves the city (M1). A panel only becomes scrollable once it sits at its snap point.
- **Rejected:** the proposed "ignore under 24px" rule, because it clips content.
- **Cues:** while there's more below, show a bottom fade with "More ↓", and a "Next: [stop] →" link at the panel's foot.
- **Keyboard and screen reader:** an overflowing panel gets `tabindex=0`, a region role and a label. A jump moves focus to the panel or project article, and an arrow jump takes focus with it.
- **Scroll position:** reset a panel's inner scroll only once it is well off screen.
- **Rejected:** `overscroll-behavior: contain` on panels (R4's Round-1 idea, withdrawn). It turns every tall sheet into a dead end. Keep today's hand-off to the next stop.

### 8. Long jumps dissolve instead of sweeping (A11Y-05, M8, R15)

- **Dissolve:** any jump longer than about 1.5 screens fades out and back in, instead of a ten-screen, 1.6s glide with full motion blur.
- **Motion blur:** stays only on the visitor's own wheel, trackpad or touch scrolling (Richard's item 3 kept).
- **Load order:** load the target stop's painting tile first, so nothing is blank for seconds on slow networks.

### 9. One even clear-glass tint that passes contrast (V4, A11Y-09, V15)

- **Tint:** replace the blurred patch behind each text block, which reads as smudges or grime by day, with one even, light tint across the panel. It's also cheaper on a phone's graphics chip.
- **Fallback:** if any sky fails 4.5:1, use much softer, larger shades.
- **Contact:** move Contact's warm light off its text column. Its "Other ways to reach me" heading is 3.67:1 at night today. Remove its cyan top border.
- **Check:** rerun the 20-sky contrast sweep against the clear glass.

### 10. Experience: friendlier and wider (V9, A11Y-15, R9)

- **Width:** wider, per Richard's item.
- **Badges:** drop the boxed all-caps WORK / EDUCATION / CURRENT badges. Keep "Current" as a word (dot alone is a colour-only cue), and give the labels separators so a screen reader doesn't read "Work Current".
- **Progress:** the bar reads "Projects · 2 of 5 Empyrean" and "Experience". Keep the bar's "03 / 06" counter.

- One warm accent, only on the primary action.
- The metro line filling per stop.
- The city dimming behind the menu and résumé.
- Fast first paint: 1.4s on slow 4G, 743 KB for the whole journey.
- Smooth scrolling (p95 about 17ms even on a slowed CPU).
- Weather failing gracefully.
- 44px targets everywhere.
- Clean landmarks and headings.
- Still view and reduced-motion handling.
- Tab bringing off-screen stops into view.
- The no-JS stacked page.
- The résumé viewer.
